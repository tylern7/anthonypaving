Our history

Three generations of Anthony paving

Anthony Paving Co. Ltd. has been family-owned and operated since 1956. Founded by Phil Anthony Sr., the company was built on exceptional workmanship and customer service. That commitment continues under Phil Anthony Jr., with the next generation Phil III and Lucas carrying on the family legacy.

Over the years the company has built a loyal client base of plaza owners, commercial property operators, property managers, builders and homeowners, with a strong volume of referrals.
